📱 Android & Mac Connection Checklist + Zero-Picker Setup
  • Disconnect other devices first: Stop controller.py on your Mac and unpair the bulb from Android/macOS System Bluetooth Settings (BLE bulbs stop advertising while connected anywhere else).
  • Android Location Toggle ON: On Android Chrome, grant Nearby Devices + Location permissions and ensure the phone's system Location (GPS) switch is turned ON while scanning.
